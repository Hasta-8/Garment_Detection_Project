Sleeve and pattern labeller

Sleeve (keys 1–5)

Pattern (click; P = plain, S = striped, C = checked)

Label the main garment in the photo (the one the product is about, not other clothes the model wears). Sleeve: short = above the elbow; three quarter = between elbow and wrist; long = to the wrist. Pattern: the dominant surface pattern. "plain" = one colour, no print. A small logo or text on a plain garment = "graphic print" only if it is the main feature, otherwise "plain". Use can't tell when the photo doesn't show it; those are left out of scoring. It saves when both are chosen and moves to the next photo.
