Scores are a softmax over each attribute's fixed list of candidates: how much closer the photo is to one label's text than to the others, not a probability of being right. Measured on catalogue photos, category is right about 3 times in 4 and colour about 3 in 5 (see "Measured accuracy").

Title and description are filled in from the labels by a fixed template; no language model writes them. "Gender section" is the catalogue section a garment is styled for, inferred from the image, not a fact about any person.
